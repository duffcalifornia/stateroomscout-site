Two kinds of alerts

Stateroom alerts

Watch the specific rooms you want: the one next to family, a connecting pair, a better deck. Rooms come back when other guests cancel or change, and they go fast, so we check every 5 minutes and alert you as soon as one opens for your party size.

Price drop alerts

Tell us what you paid for each stateroom you booked. If Disney's price for its category and party drops below it, we'll let you know, so you can ask Disney or your travel agent to reprice before final payment. Booked with a placeholder discount? We account for it.

Questions

Do I need an account?

No. Your Room Pass license key is your login. Enter it on the My Alerts page and your browser remembers it. On a new device, enter it again: your alerts are saved with the key, not the browser.

How do the notifications reach my phone?

Through ntfy, a free notification app for iPhone and Android. You pick a topic name (we suggest a random one), subscribe to it in the app, and our alerts arrive as push notifications. There's a test button to check it works.

Does it work if my browser is closed?

Yes. The checking happens on our servers, around the clock. You only need the website to set up or change your alerts.

What if I picked the wrong cruise?

Each Room Pass can change its sailing once, for a wrong pick or a change of plans. After that change, the new sailing is final.

How do price drop alerts work with a placeholder discount?

Discounts apply to the fare, not taxes, so we work out your price before the discount and alert you when Disney's price falls below that. If you're not sure of your discount percentage, leave it blank and we'll alert you when the price falls below what you paid.

Does a room alert guarantee I can get the room?

No. We tell you the room is available; booking it is up to you (or your travel agent), and popular rooms can go again within minutes.
